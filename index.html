How to plant

  1. Point your phone at the ground
  2. Tap once to grow a Malaysia tree grove
  3. Watch temp, CO₂, and AQI update on the dashboard
  4. Tap again to plant more clusters

How it works

Leaf surface area, waxy surfaces, hairy textures, and leaf density are the main factors that help trees capture haze particles. This can improve local air quality over time.

For it to be effective, the trees in one area need to be dense and close together. The following trees are used because they have the factors that help reduce haze particles.

  • Rhu — Casuarina equisetifolia
  • Angsana — Pterocarpus indicus
  • Kelat jambu — Syzygium grande
  • Penaga lilin — Mesua ferrea
  • Teh-tehan — Acalypha siamensis
